# Third-party sources

This repository contains course-project wrappers, numerical checks, recorded
measurements, and illustrative outputs. Official pretrained implementations,
checkpoints, and datasets are downloaded from their providers rather than
redistributed here. Exact revisions and SHA256 values are in resources.lock.json.

- UniDepth / UniDepthV2: https://github.com/lpiccinelli-eth/UniDepth (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- Depth Anything V2: https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2.
- Video Depth Anything: https://github.com/DepthAnything/Video-Depth-Anything.
- NYU Depth V2: https://cs.nyu.edu/~fergus/datasets/nyu_depth_v2.html.
- Middlebury: https://vision.middlebury.edu/stereo/submit3/.
- CVPR author kit, downloaded for later report preparation: https://github.com/cvpr-org/author-kit.

The two Depth Anything Small checkpoints used here are Apache 2.0; other sizes
may have different terms. Preserve the upstream notices in downloaded code and
observe each provider's model and dataset terms. The rollercoaster clip is the
official Video Depth Anything example. The videos and NYU figures here are limited
illustrative outputs derived from those sources. Bibliographic entries are in
docs/references.bib. No independent license is granted over upstream assets.
